SOPEX ADVISORY
Execution clarity — conversation guide

A worksheet for an initial leadership discussion.

1. WHERE DO YOU FEEL FRICTION?
Describe a recent example of work taking more effort than expected.
Which teams, tools, and decisions were involved?

Notes:

2. WHAT HAPPENS IN PRACTICE?
Compare the intended process with what actually happened.
Where did someone have to step in or work around the system?

Notes:

3. WHERE IS VALUE LEAKING?
Consider rework, waiting time, repeated handovers, missed opportunities,
and effort that does not improve the customer outcome.
What existing evidence could help you understand the scale?

Notes:

4. WHO OWNS THE NEXT DECISION?
Are responsibilities and decision rights understood across teams?
Which decisions depend on a particular individual?

Notes:

5. WHAT WOULD A BETTER OUTCOME LOOK LIKE?
Choose one concrete improvement you would like to see.
What could you observe or measure to know it was working?
What remains uncertain before choosing an intervention?

Notes:

Use this guide to frame a discussion. It is not a scored assessment.
A focused diagnostic can help validate assumptions and agree priorities.
